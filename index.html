FIRST PLACE · SINGAPORE 2024

The Smorphi Challenge

At the James Dyson Foundation's RoboRoarZ International in Singapore, Rayan and his team had to code a virtual and real smorphi to light up when certain QR codes are shown and to navigate a race track. Nearly 300 competitors in 48 teams from five countries took part. Rayan's name is on both podium certificates: first place in the Smorphi Challenge and third place in the Smorphi Imaginary Challenge.

03 / THE PATH

Kindergarten to Class 12.

A history of projects from the kindergarden to his  final school year

  1. Kindergarten

    Vibration controlled robot

    His first machine shook itself across the floor. His first machine consisted of 2 motors vibration to move the robot

  2. Early years

    Old toys, new machines

    He stripped broken toys for motors and gears, rebuilt them into new machines and wired them up to make something new.

  3. Primary

    LEGO creations and money-dispenser machines

    In his early days just like any kid loved lego and used it to make a vast number of creation from lego cars to relatively big lego machines.He also made a simple cash dispenser for toy money for monopoly.

  4. Class 4

    ClawBot wins the Raman Award

    A four-wheeled machine with a front claw that is controlled using wired remote and grips them with a single axis of rotation claw.

  5. Jan 2021

    First place, RoboRAVE International online

    He won his first championship in the roborave international competition The same year: a cardboard humanoid with three-joint arms and a four wheel drive 

  6. Apr 2021

    First experiments with flight

    During covid-19 he tried to make rc planes with Cardboard, launched by hand, this  started of a line that runs through RC aircraft to drones.

  7. Middle school

    From bricks to microcontrollers

    LEGO Technic gives way to Arduino: a Wi-Fi rover, gesture control, a servo arm and a four-wheel-drive chassis.

  8. Class 9

    Flight, Aero Fest and Scientia

    He tried to make a ekranoplan; his team's report on high-altitude platform systems was selected among the top 20 in India for CSIR-NAL's national Aero Fest 2023, where they took 2nd in the cultural competition; a rover model goes to Scientia.

  9. Class 9

    Third overall, World Scholar's Cup

    Debate, collaborative writing and quiz rounds at the 2023 Kochi Round, Senior Division.

  10. Jul 2024

    Two podiums in Singapore

    1st and 3rd at RoboRoarZ International with a camera-guided, QR-reading rover.

  11. 2024–25

    IIT Bombay finalist and CBSE nationals

    Top 14 of 236 teams at e-Yantra; EcoGrow 4.0 selected for the national exhibition in New Delhi.

  12. 2025

    A turret that follows your hand

    2nd place with a gyroscope-steered turret, and 2nd with an SDG model city.

  13. 2026

    Neural networks enter the toolkit

    2nd at the Praudyogiki tinkering challenge, 3rd in PyTorch relay coding, 3rd at Neural Nexus.
